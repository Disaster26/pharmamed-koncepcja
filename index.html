Tailor-made regulatory solutions, not a one-size procedure

My skills and knowledge were shaped by 20 years in the pharmaceutical industry. After a chemistry degree I spent three years in R&D at a Polish pharmaceutical company, where I moved an anxiolytic active substance from lab to production scale - and that is how I met regulatory CMC work.

Then came years in regulatory affairs at several pharmaceutical companies, most recently as Department Regulatory Affairs Manager at Fresenius Kabi Polska. Today I work as an independent consultant for clients who need a partner that knows Polish and EU law. In doubtful cases I rely on a scientific basis.

Offer

The full range of services from registration to maintenance of your products - whether your company is just starting or has been on the market for years.

Pre-registration and registration

  • Planning the registration strategy, taking legal elements into account
  • Reviewing registration possibilities and choosing the optimal procedure
  • Audit of registration documentation and readiness to start the procedure
  • Expert reports - Quality Overall Summary (Module 2.3)
  • Compilation of the chemical and pharmaceutical part of the dossier (Module 3)
  • Organising and preparing pre-submission meetings
  • Preparing and translating Product Information (SmPC, PIL, labels) according to QRD templates
  • Bridging reports with leaflet readability tests
  • Conducting national, MRP and DCP procedures
  • Filing the application
    • Module 1 and submission to local authorities
    • Monitoring the process and active dialogue with the authorities
    • Support with answers to questions raised during the procedure

Post-registration

  • Monitoring all regulatory processes: variations of all types, renewals, MA holder changes, notifications, MA withdrawals
  • Planning the strategy for post-registration variations
  • Filing variations
    • Completing variation documentation and submission
    • Monitoring the procedure and dialogue with local authorities
    • Support with answers to questions
  • Communication with authorities: first placing on the market, temporary cessation and re-placement, sunset clause exemption, annual fees
  • Re-formatting the dossier to CTD / NeeS
  • Development and adjustment of SmPC, PIL and labels
  • Verification of packaging and leaflet artwork against local requirements

Medical devices

  • Polish translation of the Instructions for Use and labelling
  • Notification of a medical device to the local authority (URPL)

Your local partner in Poland

Considering registration of a medicinal product, a medical device or FSMP, and looking for a partner in Poland you can safely entrust with confidential information? I know and understand the requirements of the regulators here.

My office is in Warsaw, close to the Polish Agency. Grant me power of attorney for local representation, and I will file applications, monitor the processes and contact the Agency on your behalf - so your company can focus on the business.
